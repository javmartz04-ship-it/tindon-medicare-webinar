The plan that was right for you in 2026 may not be right in 2027.

Same plan name. Different plan. Here's what's moving underneath it.

01

Every plan resets on January 1.

Plans re-file their benefits with Medicare every single year. Premiums, drug lists, doctor networks and copays can all change, and most people never check.

02

The help on drug plan premiums is ending.

The federal program that held standalone drug plan premiums down ends after 2026. If you have a Supplement plus a separate drug plan, this is a must-shop year.

03

A $0 premium doesn't mean $0 cost.

Advantage premiums are down on average, but out-of-pocket maximums went up. The typical in-network max moves from $6,500 to $7,100.

Most people don't overpay because they chose the wrong plan.

They overpay because they never checked again.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the webinar really free?

Yes. The webinar is free, and so is the one-on-one plan review afterward if you want one.

Do I have to change my plan?

No. Plenty of people learn their plan is still the right fit. The point is to know for sure before December 7, while you can still make a change.

What should I have handy?

Your Annual Notice of Change letter, your list of prescriptions, and your list of doctors. If you can't find the letter, bring your plan name and we'll look it up together on your review.
