Frequently Asked Questions

Free  ·  About 20 minutes  ·  Live Q&A

Is the webinar really free?

Yes. The webinar is free, and so is the one-on-one plan review afterward if you want one.

Do I have to change my plan?

No. Plenty of people learn their plan is still the right fit. The point is to know for sure before December 7, while you can still make a change.

What should I have handy?

Your Annual Notice of Change letter, your list of prescriptions, and your list of doctors. If you can't find the letter, bring your plan name.
